GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions.

Who is this home for?

Adults ages 55+ seeking a furnished private bedroom in a shared Detroit home, who manage their daily activities independently.

Is this assisted living?

No. This is independent shared housing. There are no caregivers or staff providing supervision, medical services, meals, or personal care.

What will I need to provide?

Residents are responsible for their own food, medications, transportation, and personal-care needs.

Are rooms available now?

Private room availability is coming soon. Complete the interest form to connect about availability, room rates, and next steps.

Can a family member inquire?

Yes. A family member, friend, or referral partner can submit an inquiry on behalf of a prospective resident, with their permission.
